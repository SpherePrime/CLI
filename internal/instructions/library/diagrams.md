# Explaining mechanisms with diagrams

Draw the mechanism the reader needs to understand.
Show flows, components, transitions and boundaries changed by decisions. Label arrows with operations and depict differing edges for alternatives. Keep complexity proportional, align elements and use short labels. Use formats supported by the destination. Inline SVG needs viewBox, responsive sizing, theme-aware currentColor, local markers and accessible labels plus captions. Keep graphics self-contained without executable content or unnecessary resources.
