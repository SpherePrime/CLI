# Making charts legible and interactive

Make visual encodings legible and interactions meaningful.
Label axes and units, format consistently and directly label when useful. Keep text readable and marks distinguishable under color-vision differences; shape and texture must convey meaning. Tooltips expose values and context with comparable keyboard access. Keep colors and scales stable under filtering; indicate active filters and explain empty results. Avoid decorative distortion, excessive colors and misleading precision. Verify mobile, theme and export behavior.
