# Arctic Frost

A cool and crisp winter-inspired theme that conveys clarity, precision, and professionalism.

## Color Palette

- **Ice Blue**: `#d4e4f7` - Light backgrounds and highlights
- **Steel Blue**: `#4a6fa5` - Primary accent color
- **Silver**: `#c0c0c0` - Metallic accent elements
- **Crisp White**: `#fafafa` - Clean backgrounds and text

## Typography

- **Headers**: DejaVu Sans Bold
- **Body Text**: DejaVu Sans

## Best Used For

Healthcare presentations, technology solutions, winter sports, clean tech, pharmaceutical content.

## Verified contrast pairs

Measured with `scripts/contrast.mjs` (WCAG 2.2). Use only these pairs for text and controls.

**Body text (≥ 4.5:1)**

- Steel Blue `#4a6fa5` + Crisp White `#fafafa` — 4.90:1

**Large text ≥ 24px, icons, borders, focus rings (3–4.5:1)**

- Ice Blue `#d4e4f7` + Steel Blue `#4a6fa5` — 3.95:1

**Decoration only (fails 3:1 with every other swatch)**

- Silver `#c0c0c0`

Pairs not listed fail WCAG for text. Derive tints and shades in OKLCH and re-run the checker before adding new pairs.
