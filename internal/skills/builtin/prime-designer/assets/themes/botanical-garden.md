# Botanical Garden

A fresh and organic theme featuring vibrant garden-inspired colors for lively presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Fern Green**: `#4a7c59` - Rich natural green
- **Marigold**: `#f9a620` - Bright floral accent
- **Terracotta**: `#b7472a` - Earthy warm tone
- **Cream**: `#f5f3ed` - Soft neutral backgrounds

## Typography

- **Headers**: DejaVu Serif Bold
- **Body Text**: DejaVu Sans

## Best Used For

Garden centers, food presentations, farm-to-table content, botanical brands, natural products.

## Verified contrast pairs

Measured with `scripts/contrast.mjs` (WCAG 2.2). Use only these pairs for text and controls.

**Body text (≥ 4.5:1)**

- Terracotta `#b7472a` + Cream `#f5f3ed` — 4.79:1

**Large text ≥ 24px, icons, borders, focus rings (3–4.5:1)**

- Fern Green `#4a7c59` + Cream `#f5f3ed` — 4.38:1

**Decoration only (fails 3:1 with every other swatch)**

- Marigold `#f9a620`

Pairs not listed fail WCAG for text. Derive tints and shades in OKLCH and re-run the checker before adding new pairs.
