# Forest Canopy

A natural and grounded theme featuring earth tones inspired by dense forest environments.

## Color Palette

- **Forest Green**: `#2d4a2b` - Primary dark green
- **Sage**: `#7d8471` - Muted green accent
- **Olive**: `#a4ac86` - Light accent color
- **Ivory**: `#faf9f6` - Backgrounds and text

## Typography

- **Headers**: FreeSerif Bold
- **Body Text**: FreeSans

## Best Used For

Environmental presentations, sustainability reports, outdoor brands, wellness content, organic products.

## Verified contrast pairs

Measured with `scripts/contrast.mjs` (WCAG 2.2). Use only these pairs for text and controls.

**Body text (≥ 4.5:1)**

- Forest Green `#2d4a2b` + Ivory `#faf9f6` — 9.38:1

**Large text ≥ 24px, icons, borders, focus rings (3–4.5:1)**

- Forest Green `#2d4a2b` + Olive `#a4ac86` — 4.15:1
- Sage `#7d8471` + Ivory `#faf9f6` — 3.69:1

**Decoration only (fails 3:1 with every other swatch)**

- None.

Pairs not listed fail WCAG for text. Derive tints and shades in OKLCH and re-run the checker before adding new pairs.
