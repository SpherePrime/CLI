# Sunset Boulevard

A warm and vibrant theme inspired by golden hour sunsets, perfect for energetic and creative presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Burnt Orange**: `#e76f51` - Primary accent color
- **Coral**: `#f4a261` - Secondary warm accent
- **Warm Sand**: `#e9c46a` - Highlighting and backgrounds
- **Deep Purple**: `#264653` - Dark contrast and text

## Typography

- **Headers**: DejaVu Serif Bold
- **Body Text**: DejaVu Sans

## Best Used For

Creative pitches, marketing presentations, lifestyle brands, event promotions, inspirational content.

## Verified contrast pairs

Measured with `scripts/contrast.mjs` (WCAG 2.2). Use only these pairs for text and controls.

**Body text (≥ 4.5:1)**

- Warm Sand `#e9c46a` + Deep Purple `#264653` — 6.03:1
- Coral `#f4a261` + Deep Purple `#264653` — 4.89:1

**Large text ≥ 24px, icons, borders, focus rings (3–4.5:1)**

- Burnt Orange `#e76f51` + Deep Purple `#264653` — 3.26:1

**Decoration only (fails 3:1 with every other swatch)**

- None.

Pairs not listed fail WCAG for text. Derive tints and shades in OKLCH and re-run the checker before adding new pairs.
