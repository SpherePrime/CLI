# Tech Innovation

A bold and modern theme with high-contrast colors perfect for cutting-edge technology presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Electric Blue**: `#0066ff` - Vibrant primary accent
- **Neon Cyan**: `#00ffff` - Bright highlight color
- **Dark Gray**: `#1e1e1e` - Deep backgrounds
- **White**: `#ffffff` - Clean text and contrast

## Typography

- **Headers**: DejaVu Sans Bold
- **Body Text**: DejaVu Sans

## Best Used For

Tech startups, software launches, innovation showcases, AI/ML presentations, digital transformation content.

## Verified contrast pairs

Measured with `scripts/contrast.mjs` (WCAG 2.2). Use only these pairs for text and controls.

**Body text (≥ 4.5:1)**

- Dark Gray `#1e1e1e` + White `#ffffff` — 16.67:1
- Neon Cyan `#00ffff` + Dark Gray `#1e1e1e` — 13.30:1
- Electric Blue `#0066ff` + White `#ffffff` — 4.83:1

**Large text ≥ 24px, icons, borders, focus rings (3–4.5:1)**

- Electric Blue `#0066ff` + Neon Cyan `#00ffff` — 3.85:1
- Electric Blue `#0066ff` + Dark Gray `#1e1e1e` — 3.45:1

**Decoration only (fails 3:1 with every other swatch)**

- None.

Pairs not listed fail WCAG for text. Derive tints and shades in OKLCH and re-run the checker before adding new pairs.
